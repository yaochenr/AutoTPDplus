Agentic Literature Extraction Augments Targeted Protein Degradation Databases

Yaochen Rao1, Farzaneh Jalalypour1, N. M. Anoop Krishnan2, Rocío Mercado1,3*

1Chalmers University of Technology and University of Gothenburg  ·  2Yardi School of Artificial Intelligence, IIT Delhi  ·  3SciLifeLab, Gothenburg  ·  *Corresponding author

From papers to records, every month.

AutoTPD+ is a living database of PROTAC and molecular glue degradation assays. An LLM workflow mines open-access PubMed Central papers for each compound's target, recruiter, assay, cell line and DC50/Dmax values. PROTAC-DB and MolGlueDB records cover papers without open full text. A monthly search adds new papers.
